How to measure
  1. Hip: measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, with feet together.
  2. Crotch depth (body rise): sit on a flat chair and measure from the side of your waist down to the seat surface.
  3. Waist: measure around your natural waist, or where you wear your trousers.
  4. Inseam: measure from the crotch down the inside of the leg to the ankle bone.
  5. Check it: lay a well-fitting pair of trousers flat and measure the front rise and back rise from the crotch seam up to the top of the waistband.

Saved history

    Crotch length is the full seam from the front of the waistband, through the crotch, to the back of the waistband. These figures are estimates from standard pattern-making proportions. Real garments vary with fabric, stretch and style.
